Care and storage

Cleaning

Hand wash only, with lukewarm water, mild dish soap and a non-abrasive sponge. The mold is not dishwasher safe.

Storage

Keep the mold in a clean, dry place, away from anything that could cut or deform it.

Precautions

  • Never place the mold in the oven.
  • Freezer use only.
  • Do not use sharp objects on the silicone.
  • Not dishwasher safe.
